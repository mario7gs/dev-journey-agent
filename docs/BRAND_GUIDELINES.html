03 · Logo

Direction made visible

Meaning: the route starts from a grounded requirement, passes through verified nodes, and rises toward a deliverable.

Usage rules

  • Keep the mark on Ink or Paper with clear space around it.
  • Use the full lockup in report headers and portfolio surfaces.
  • Use the standalone mark for compact navigation, favicons, and slide corners.
  • Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, add effects, or place it over busy imagery.

06 · Voice

Clear, evidence-led, and calm

Prefer specific statements over hype. Use state labels such as approved, needs input, requirement, and test when they help the reader understand the work.

The visual language should make the project's auditability feel approachable—not bureaucratic.
